# Poll Everywhere activities: feedback, frequency response, and Bode plots

These nine multiple-choice questions are organized into three coherent sets. Each set contains three activities and is designed to take approximately 8–10 minutes, including individual responses, peer discussion, and instructor debriefing.

The public page intentionally does not identify the correct answers. Instructors should use the separate Poll Everywhere CSV import files, which contain the correct-answer markers required by the importer.

Do not use all nine activities in one 75-minute meeting. Use the three-question set that matches the day's lesson.

---

## Set 1: Feedback control and applications

### Activity 1.1 — Close the loop correctly

For a standard negative-feedback system with forward-path transfer function $G(s)$ and feedback-path transfer function $H(s)$, what is the closed-loop transfer function $Y(s)/R(s)$?

- $G(s)/[1-G(s)H(s)]$
- $G(s)/[1+G(s)H(s)]$
- $H(s)/[1+G(s)]$
- $G(s)H(s)$

### Activity 1.2 — Predict the closed-loop response from its poles

A unity-feedback system has

$$
G(s)=\frac{4}{s(s+2)}.
$$

Which description of the closed-loop response is correct?

- Stable and underdamped, with poles at $-1\pm j\sqrt3$
- Stable and overdamped, with poles at $0$ and $-2$
- Unstable because the closed-loop poles are complex
- Marginally stable, with poles at $\pm j2$

### Activity 1.3 — Positive feedback and stability

A unity positive-feedback system has

$$
G(s)=\frac{K}{s+2},
\qquad K>0.
$$

For which values of $K$ is the closed-loop system stable?

- $0<K<2$
- $K=2$ only
- $K>2$
- Every $K>0$, because the open-loop pole is at $-2$

## Set 2: Frequency response

### Activity 2.1 — Apply magnitude and phase

Suppose

$$
H(j3)=0.4e^{-j\pi/6}
$$

and

$$
x(t)=5\cos(3t+\pi/4).
$$

What is the sinusoidal steady-state output?

- $2\cos(3t+\pi/12)$
- $2\cos(3t+5\pi/12)$
- $5\cos(3t+\pi/12)$
- $0.4\cos(3t-\pi/6)$

### Activity 2.2 — Separate transient from steady state

The zero-state response is

$$
y(t)=[-2.4e^{-4t}+2.4\cos(2t)+1.2\sin(2t)]u(t).
$$

Which term is the transient generated by the system pole?

- $-2.4e^{-4t}$
- $2.4\cos(2t)$
- $1.2\sin(2t)$
- Both sinusoidal terms

### Activity 2.3 — Interpret nonzero initial conditions

A stable LTI system has nonzero initial conditions and is driven by $A\cos(\omega t)u(t)$. Which statement is correct?

- Initial conditions change the sinusoidal steady-state frequency.
- $H(j\omega)$ is invalid whenever initial conditions are nonzero.
- Initial conditions add a zero-input transient, but the long-term sinusoidal response is still predicted by $H(j\omega)$.
- The zero-input response becomes part of the transfer function.

## Set 3: Bode plots

### Activity 3.1 — Interpret the corner-frequency symbol

For

$$
H(s)=\frac{1}{1+s/10},
$$

what does $\omega_p=10$ mean?

- The pole is at $s=+10$, and the corner frequency is $10$ Hz.
- The pole is at $s=-10$, and the corner frequency is $10$ rad/s.
- The pole is at $s=-0.1$, and the corner frequency is $0.1$ rad/s.
- The magnitude is always $10$ dB.

### Activity 3.2 — Accumulate Bode slopes

Consider

$$
H(s)=\frac{10(1+s/10)}{(1+s)(1+s/100)}.
$$

What is the asymptotic magnitude slope for $10<\omega<100$ rad/s?

- $+20$ dB/decade
- $0$ dB/decade
- $-20$ dB/decade
- $-40$ dB/decade

### Activity 3.3 — Read a Bode plot as a sinusoidal response

At $\omega=5$ rad/s, a Bode plot gives a magnitude of $-6$ dB and a phase of $-30^\circ$. For

$$
x(t)=4\cos(5t+10^\circ),
$$

what is the approximate steady-state output?

- $2\cos(5t-20^\circ)$
- $-2\cos(5t-30^\circ)$
- $4\cos(5t-20^\circ)$
- $0.5\cos(5t+40^\circ)$
